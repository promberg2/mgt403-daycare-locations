The BLS OEWS area files (oesm15ma.zip ... oesm22ma.zip, 275 MB) are not included. `scripts/02_get_oews.py` downloads them (set BLS_CONTACT_EMAIL to your own e-mail), or download them manually from https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/ into this folder. Their SHA-256 checksums are in `data/raw_checksums.sha256`. The extracted wages used by all later steps are in `data/interim/`.
